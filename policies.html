FAPO Warranty Policy

Based on the FAPOMOTO warranty standard, we prepared the following warranty handling rules for FAPO Polska customers.

Warranty is an additional route and does not limit a consumer's statutory rights for lack of conformity of goods with the contract. The customer may choose whether to use the statutory complaint route or warranty, if warranty was granted.

Coverage

  • Standard warranty: 12 months from purchase date.
  • Coverage includes defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
  • Products eligible for the Warranty Extension Program may receive up to 24 months of coverage.

Required claim documents

  • Data allowing us to identify the purchase, such as order number, payment confirmation, order e-mail or invoice.
  • Issue documentation, such as photos or video, is helpful and speeds up verification, but is not an automatic condition for accepting a complaint.
  • Short description of when and how the issue occurred.

In most cases, returning the defective product is not required at the initial verification stage.

Claim process

  1. Send the claim to office@fapomoto.pl with order number, product details and photos/videos.
  2. Claim review time is up to 2 business days.
  3. We may request additional information to verify the defect and determine the root cause.
  4. After approval, we ship replacement parts or a replacement product at service discretion.

What is not covered

  • Damage caused by misuse, overload, collisions, abuse or neglect.
  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Unauthorized modifications or repairs.
  • Products purchased outside authorized sales channels.
  • Damage caused by improper installation or maintenance.

Limitation of liability

  • To the extent permitted by law, warranty liability may be limited to repair or replacement of the defective product.
  • Indirect costs are not automatically reimbursed, including labor, install/removal, towing, downtime and loss of vehicle use.
  • Warranty limitations do not exclude statutory consumer rights or liability for lack of conformity of goods with the contract.

FAPO Return Policy

This policy separates the statutory 14-day withdrawal right, the voluntary FAPOMOTO return program up to 30 days, and complaints for lack of conformity of goods with the contract.

Policy summary

  • A consumer may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days of receiving the goods, without giving a reason.
  • From day 15 to day 30, FAPOMOTO may accept a voluntary return of unused, uninstalled, complete and undamaged goods.
  • Contact before shipping the item back helps us assign an RA number and process the case faster, but lack of RA does not limit statutory consumer rights.
  • For statutory withdrawal, the refund is made no later than 14 days after receiving the withdrawal statement, with the possibility to withhold it until we receive the goods or proof of return shipment.

Return eligibility

Statutory withdrawal within 14 days:

  • Original packaging is recommended if the customer has it, but it is not a condition of exercising the statutory withdrawal right unless the packaging was the sold item or an essential part of the goods.
  • The customer is responsible for any reduced value caused by handling beyond what is necessary to establish the nature, characteristics and functioning of the goods.

Complaints and fulfilment errors:

  • Defective or non-conforming products may be reported to FAPOMOTO; photos/video help, but are not an automatic condition for accepting a complaint.
  • Incorrect items shipped by FAPO.
  • Missing components should be reported as soon as possible, preferably within 5 days of delivery; this timing is operational and does not limit statutory customer rights.

Return process (RA)

  1. Send a clear withdrawal statement or return request to office@fapomoto.pl, including data that lets us identify the order.
  2. After receiving the request, we will send return instructions and, where possible, a Return Authorization (RA) number.
  3. For statutory withdrawal, send the goods back without delay and no later than 14 days after submitting the withdrawal statement; for the voluntary day 15-30 program, follow the shipping instructions provided by FAPOMOTO.

The RA number helps us identify the parcel and case. Lack of RA does not deprive a consumer of an effectively exercised withdrawal right or the right to file a complaint.

Fees and refunds

  • Statutory consumer withdrawal: no restocking fee is charged. The customer pays the direct return shipping cost unless FAPOMOTO agrees to cover it or the law provides otherwise.
  • Defective item / FAPO shipping error: a justified consumer complaint is handled without customer cost under the applicable legal rules.
  • Voluntary day 15-30 return: return shipping cost is paid by the customer unless FAPOMOTO decides otherwise; the program applies to unused, uninstalled, complete and undamaged goods.
  • Return inspection: within 3-5 business days after receipt.
  • Refund after withdrawal: within 14 days of receiving the withdrawal statement, with the possibility to withhold it until we receive the goods or proof of shipment.
  • Refund to original payment method (typically 5-10 business days on payment provider side).

After 30 days and critical conditions

  • Defects or non-conformities discovered after 30 days may be reported as a statutory complaint for lack of conformity of goods with the contract or under warranty, if granted.
  • For visible transport damage, please prepare a carrier damage report and take photos where possible. Lack of a damage report does not exclude the possibility to file a complaint with FAPOMOTO.
  • Order cancellation before shipment is free; post-shipment cancellation is treated as a return.
  • Liability limitations do not exclude statutory consumer rights or rights related to lack of conformity of goods with the contract.

FAPO Shipping Policy

Based on FAPOMOTO shipping standards, we apply the following shipping handling rules for orders managed by FAPO Polska.

Order processing

  • Orders are processed within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays).
  • Once shipped, the customer receives a tracking number.
  • Estimated delivery after dispatch: 2-7 business days (depending on carrier and delivery zone).

Delivery times may vary due to carrier delays, weather conditions, or incorrect addresses.

Shipping methods and restrictions

  • Shipping is handled by partner couriers (including FedEx, UPS, DHL), depending on region and package size.
  • We do not ship to PO Boxes.
  • Addresses outside supported shipping zones may result in order cancellation and full refund.

Tracking and undelivered packages

  1. Log in to your customer account and open order history.
  2. Select the order number to view tracking information and shipment status.
  3. If a package has not arrived within 5 business days after the estimated delivery date, check tracking and contact FAPOMOTO; contacting the carrier may help, but it does not replace reporting the issue to the seller.

For delivery methods offered by FAPOMOTO, we handle the case toward the customer and pursue any carrier claim on our side where needed.

Damaged or lost packages

  • Damaged package: keep all packaging and contents, take photos, and email office@fapomoto.pl as soon as possible. This timing is operational and does not limit statutory consumer rights.
  • Lost package: contact us after at least 5 business days past the estimated delivery date.
  • Every case should include order number and complete supporting details.
